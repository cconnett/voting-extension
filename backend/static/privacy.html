What we store

From voters

  • An opaque identifier provided to us by Twitch. We use it to prevent duplicate voting. When the poll closes, we delete the opaque identifier.
  • The ordering and timestamp of your last submitted ballot.

From broadcasters

  • Your channel name and the candidate options you provide in polls you create.

Sharing

  • Poll results are public. They are calculated from aggregated ballot data.
Raw ballot data is never shared.
